Questions

Before you ask.

What do you need from me to start?

A CAD part (STEP, IGES or Parasolid) and a sentence about what you're simulating — flow conditions, the quantities you care about, any constraints. That's enough for a first mesh.

Which solvers can you export to?

OpenFOAM, ANSYS Fluent and Abaqus today, with CGNS and Gmsh interchange. If you run something else, tell us — formats are the easy part.

Is my geometry kept private?

Yes. Parts are handled under NDA and never used to train shared models without explicit agreement. Aerospace IP is the whole game.

Do engineers stay in control?

Always. Hexera automates the setup and labour, not the judgment — you approve the simulation intent, and every decision the agent makes is logged and reported.

How fast is a typical turnaround?

Most parts that took 6–8 days of manual preprocessing come back solver-ready in hours, quality-checked, with a written report.
