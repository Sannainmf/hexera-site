Who we are

Meshing is the bottleneck. We're removing it.

Every CFD team loses days to preprocessing — repairing geometry, hand-building boundary layers, babysitting solvers. Hexera exists to give that week back, on every part, to every engineer.

What we believe

Five principles we mesh by.

Physics first

A pretty mesh that fails the solver is worthless. Quality metrics decide, not aesthetics.

Engineer in control

We automate the setup, never the judgment. You approve intent; the agent does the labour.

Speed is a feature

Hours, not days. Fast turnaround is what lets teams explore the design space at all.

Auditable by default

Every decision is logged and reported. No black boxes in a safety-critical workflow.

Built by operators

We've meshed in anger and shipped hardware. We build the tool we always wished we had.
