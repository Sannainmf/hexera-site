# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

CFD and aerospace simulation engineers, and the technical leads / heads of
engineering who buy tooling for them. Their context: high-stakes, deadline-bound
simulation projects (airfoils, rockets, planes, rotorcraft) where mesh generation
and preprocessing currently eat up to 80% of project time — typically 6–8 days of
specialized manual work per iteration. They are technically sophisticated,
skeptical of marketing fluff, and fluent in the vernacular of meshing (y+, cell
count, skewness, boundary layers, Reynolds number). The job to be done: stop
hand-building meshes and get simulation-ready results in hours.

## Product Purpose

Hexera automates CAD-to-mesh generation for aerospace simulation. A multi-agent AI
pipeline replaces the manual preprocessing bottleneck: one agent analyzes geometry,
one translates plain-English simulation intent into technical meshing parameters,
one autonomously writes and debugs meshing scripts, and one visually evaluates the
output the way a senior engineer would. If a mesh fails inspection, the system
self-corrects until it produces a simulation-ready result. Success: become the
default preprocessing/meshing layer for every simulation industry, starting with
American aerospace — turning 6–8 days of work into hours.

## Brand Personality

Precise, autonomous, formidable. The voice of an instrument that just works — a
wind-tunnel readout, not a sales pitch. Three words: **computational, aerodynamic,
exact.** It should evoke confidence and a slight awe at the machine doing what took
a senior team a week. Engineering-native, never consumer-cute. Speaks the
engineer's language and earns trust by demonstrating fluency, not by claiming it.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-startup landing pages: identical card grids, gradient-text headlines,
  hero-metric templates, tiny tracked eyebrows above every section.
- SaaS-cream / warm-neutral editorial looks. This is a dark, technical instrument,
  not a magazine.
- Anything that reads as "software built for the 80s" (the incumbent CFD tooling
  Hexera is replacing) — cluttered, beige, dated UI chrome.
- Looking templated or interchangeable with other AI-sim startups. The bar is "how
  was this made?", not "which AI made this?"

## Design Principles

- **Show the physics, don't claim it.** The hero should *be* CFD — a living mesh
  and flow over a real aerospace geometry — not a stock illustration of one.
- **Speak the engineer's vernacular.** Real meshing/CFD terms (y+, cell count,
  skewness, Re) used correctly are the trust signal. Specificity over adjectives.
- **Expert confidence, quiet surface.** Spend boldness on the signature canvas;
  keep typography, layout, and color disciplined around it.
- **Hours, not days.** The core promise — speed against the manual baseline —
  should be legible everywhere without being shouted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 against the obsidian background.
Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (the flow-field canvas must degrade to a
static, still-beautiful frame). Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements.
Canvas is decorative — never the sole carrier of information; all meaning also lives
in real text.
