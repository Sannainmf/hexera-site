A multi-agent pipeline takes each part from raw CAD to solver-ready — specialised agents read the geometry, mesh to intent, check their own work, and hand off.

Ingest Opens the CAD, heals gaps, tags the walls that matter.
Mesh Turns plain-language intent into real y+ and prism layers.
Verify Grades skewness and orthogonality, re-refines the failures.
Deliver Exports solver-ready to OpenFOAM, ANSYS or Abaqus.
