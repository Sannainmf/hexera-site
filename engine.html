act 01 · read

It reads the part, not a picture of it.

Two thousand scattered B-rep faces resolve into one watertight body — edges sewn, slivers healed, tolerances understood before a single cell exists.

act 02 · tag

Every surface gets a job.

Skin, leading edge, flaperon, root and tip — classified from your intent, not hand-clicked. walls · inlet · outlet · symmetry, named the way your solver expects.

act 03 · mesh

Boundary layers grown like they were drawn by hand.

Blocks split exactly at the flaperon edges, cells packed into the leading and trailing edges. Then the section fills: eight prism layers at 1.25 growth, an O-grid marching out to the far field, and a wake block trailing the TE.

act 04 · prove

It grades its own work before you see it.

A quality gate sweeps every station — skewness, orthogonality, volume ratio. What fails gets re-refined on the spot and swept again, then the report is signed.
