01 / INGEST

Read the geometry

Opens your CAD natively — heals gaps, tags walls, inlets and trailing edges.

02 / MESH

Translate intent to a mesh

Turns plain-language intent into real parameters — y+, prism layers, refinement — then writes and runs the script.

03 / VERIFY

Inspect like a reviewer

Grades every mesh on skewness, orthogonality and aspect ratio — and re-refines failures itself.

04 / DELIVER

Hand off, solver-ready

Exports to OpenFOAM, ANSYS or Abaqus with a full decision report. Days become hours.
