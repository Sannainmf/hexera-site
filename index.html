Inside the pipeline

From a single CAD part to a solver-ready mesh. Every step runs automatically, and every decision is logged.

Built by engineers. The pipeline runs end to end, so you keep the judgment and skip the busywork.

Send

  1. 1.1

    Geometry intake

    Drop a STEP, IGES or Parasolid part. Hexera reads the solid, its faces and edges.

  2. 1.2

    Simulation intent

    Describe the flow, the conditions and the quantities you care about, in plain language.

Mesh

  1. 2.1

    Builder

    Translates the intent into a meshing strategy and writes the geometry script.

  2. 2.2

    Executor

    Runs blockMesh and snappyHexMesh to generate the volume mesh.

  3. 2.3

    Reviewer

    Checks skewness, orthogonality and y+, refining locally until it passes.

Export

  1. 3.1

    Solver-ready formats

    OpenFOAM, ANSYS Fluent and Abaqus, with CGNS and Gmsh interchange.

  2. 3.2

    Quality report

    Every mesh ships with its metrics and a reproducible script you can re-run.
